Privacy Policy
Last updated: April 24, 2026

360Epoxy ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, submit a form, or opt in to receive SMS text messages from us.

1. Information We Collect
We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when you fill out forms on our website. This includes:

Contact Information: First name, last name, email address, and mobile phone number
Address Information: Street address, city, state, and ZIP code
Project Information: Project type (garage, basement, commercial, etc.), approximate square footage, desired timeline, and additional project details or notes
Consent Records: Your SMS opt-in preferences (transactional and/or marketing), the timestamp of your opt-in, and the device/browser used at the time of consent
We do not collect sensitive personal information such as Social Security numbers, financial account numbers, or payment card information through our website forms.

2. How We Use Your Information
We use the personal information we collect strictly for the following business purposes:

Providing Estimates: To prepare and deliver a free project estimate based on the details you provide
Communication: To contact you by phone call, email, or SMS regarding your project inquiry, estimate, appointment, or related promotional offers
Appointment Scheduling: To schedule, confirm, and send reminders for on-site evaluations and project appointments
Transactional SMS: If you opt in, to send transactional text messages including appointment reminders, project status updates, estimate follow-ups, and scheduling confirmations
Marketing SMS: If you opt in to SMS messaging, to send promotional text messages including special offers, seasonal discounts, new service announcements, and company updates
Customer Service: To respond to your questions, requests, and feedback
Service Improvement: To analyze how visitors use our website so we can improve our services and user experience
We will never use your personal information for purposes other than those listed above without obtaining your explicit consent first.

3. SMS Opt-In & Messaging Details
By checking the SMS consent checkbox on our website form, you expressly consent to receive text messages from 360Epoxy at the mobile phone number you provide. This opt-in may include project-related and promotional SMS messages from 360Epoxy:

Project-Related Messages:

Appointment confirmations and reminders
Project estimate follow-ups
Scheduling updates and changes
Project status notifications
Service completion confirmations
Marketing & Promotional Messages:

Seasonal promotions and special offers
New service announcements
Discounts and exclusive deals
Company news and updates
Message Frequency: Message frequency may vary. Transactional messages are sent as needed based on your project status and interactions. Marketing messages may be sent up to 4 times per month.

Message & Data Rates: Standard message and data rates may apply depending on your mobile carrier and plan. 360Epoxy is not responsible for any messaging or data charges imposed by your carrier.

How to Opt Out: You may opt out at any time by replying STOP to any text message from 360Epoxy. You will receive a one-time confirmation message, and no further messages will be sent. You can also call us at 385-558-3495 or email 360epoxyaz@gmail.com to request removal.

How to Get Help: Reply HELP to any text message for assistance, or contact us directly at 385-558-3495 or 360epoxyaz@gmail.com.

Consent Is Not Required: Opting in to SMS messaging is completely voluntary and is not a condition of purchasing any goods or services from 360Epoxy. You may request a project estimate without opting in to receive text messages.

4. Cookies & Tracking Technologies
Our website uses cookies and similar tracking technologies to improve your experience and understand how our site is used. The types of cookies and tracking we employ include:

Essential/Functional Cookies: These cookies are necessary for the basic operation of our website, including form submission functionality, page navigation, and session management. They do not collect personal information for marketing purposes.
Analytics Cookies: We may use third-party analytics services, such as Google Analytics, to collect anonymized and aggregated data about website traffic, page views, user behavior, and traffic sources. This data helps us understand how visitors interact with our website so we can make improvements. Google Analytics may use cookies to track your interactions. You can learn more about Google's practices at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
Third-Party Widgets: Our website may include embedded third-party tools such as live chat widgets, scheduling tools, or social media buttons. These tools may set their own cookies and collect data according to their own privacy policies, which are separate from ours.
Pixel/Conversion Tracking: We may use tracking pixels from advertising platforms (such as Facebook/Meta or Google Ads) to measure the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns. These pixels may collect anonymized data about your visit.
Managing Cookies: You can control and manage cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to block or delete cookies. However, disabling cookies may affect certain features of our website, such as form submission. For instructions on managing cookies, refer to your browser's help documentation.

Do Not Track: Our website currently does not respond to "Do Not Track" browser signals. However, you can manage tracking through cookie settings and browser controls as described above.

5. Data Security Practices
We take the security and protection of your personal information seriously and implement appropriate technical and organizational measures, including:

Encryption in Transit: All data submitted through our website forms is transmitted using SSL/TLS encryption (HTTPS), ensuring your information is encrypted during transmission between your browser and our servers.
Secure Data Storage: Your personal information is stored securely within our customer relationship management (CRM) platform, GoHighLevel, which maintains industry-standard security certifications and practices including SOC 2 compliance, data encryption at rest, and regular security audits.
Access Controls: Access to your personal data is restricted to authorized 360Epoxy personnel who require it to perform their job functions (e.g., preparing estimates, scheduling appointments, sending authorized communications). All personnel with access are trained on data privacy practices.
SMS Provider Security: Our SMS messaging is delivered through GoHighLevel's integrated messaging platform, which utilizes compliant carrier connections and industry-standard security protocols for message transmission.
Regular Reviews: We periodically review our data handling procedures, security measures, and access controls to ensure they remain appropriate and effective.
While we implement reasonable safeguards to protect your data, no method of electronic transmission or digital storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to protecting your information to the best of our ability and will promptly notify affected individuals in the event of a data breach as required by applicable law.
