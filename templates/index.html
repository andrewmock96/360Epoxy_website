Our Services

Garage Flooring

We use top-of-the-line polyaspartic coatings that are designed to protect, strengthen, and elevate the look of your garage floor. Unlike traditional epoxy, polyaspartic coatings cure faster, resist UV discoloration, and offer superior durability against chemicals, abrasions, and daily wear. The result is a long-lasting, glossy, and easy-to-clean surface that transforms your garage into a clean, modern, and functional space you can be proud of.

Commercial Coatings

We provide durable flooring solutions built to withstand demanding environments such as warehouses, showrooms, manufacturing facilities, and industrial workspaces. Our commercial-grade polyaspartic systems deliver exceptional strength, chemical resistance, and slip protection, ensuring a safe and long-lasting surface that stands up to heavy traffic and tough conditions. Whether you need a polished aesthetic or maximum durability, our coatings offer reliable performance you can depend on.

Custom Designs

Create a truly unique look with our wide range of design choices. With 24 different flake options and 4 base coat colors, you can tailor your floor to match any style, bold, modern, subtle, or anything in between. No matter your vision, our polyaspartic coating system gives you the flexibility to achieve a one-of-a-kind floor that is both beautiful and durable.
