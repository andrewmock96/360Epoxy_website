Premium Package

Our Premium Package offers high-quality epoxy coatings that not only protect your garage floors but also transform the space into a polished, showroom-quality area. These coatings are resistant to stains, chemicals, abrasions, and everyday wear, making them easy to clean and maintain. Ultra Package

The Ultra Package is designed for commercial and high-traffic areas such as warehouses, offices, and showrooms. Our durable epoxy solutions provide maximum surface protection while improving the overall appearance of your space. Resistant to heavy foot and vehicle traffic, spills, and impacts, this coating ensures a long-lasting, safe, and visually appealing floor that can withstand even the most demanding environments.

The Advanced Package is perfect for customers looking to create a truly unique and customized flooring solution. Basic Package

The Basic Package provides heavy-duty epoxy coatings ideal for garages, industrial spaces, and high-traffic areas. Durable and functional, it ensures your floors are easy to clean, safe, and resistant to damage, making it a reliable choice for both residential and commercial applications.
